# Planet and satellite imagery

Direct source: [NASA Earth Observatory global JPEG](https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-base/july/world.200407.3x5400x2700.jpg).

Credit: NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble: Next Generation. It is a monthly cloud-free observation composite, not a live view. Colour is an aesthetic observation composite, including lower-resolution polar data and filled gaps, rather than a scientific measurement layer.

Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio / LRO; Ernie Wright (USRA), Noah Petro (NASA/GSFC), LROC and LOLA teams. The renderer uploads bounded 1024 / 2048-wide colour and 512 / 1024-wide linear elevation textures with mip levels. Elevation enhances shading only; relief strength, lighting, rotation and Earth–Moon spacing are artistic representations, not a live orbital or terrain simulation. Observation PNGs credit these maps when their bodies are visible.

## Dawn main-belt worlds

`ceres.png` and `vesta.png` are 2048 × 1024 derivatives of the complete **clear-filter, equidistant cylindrical** HAMO mosaics linked on [DLR Ceres data](https://dawngis.dlr.de/data/Ceres/mosaic_ceres.php) and [DLR Vesta data](https://dawngis.dlr.de/data/Vesta/mosaic_vesta.php). Credit: **NASA/JPL-Caltech/UCLA/MPS/DLR/IDA**, with mosaic processing by Roatsch et al. Ceres images date to 2015; Vesta combines HAMO-1/2 in 2011/2012. Both remain monochrome with source mosaic lighting; the elliptical hi-res previews and true-colour preview are not used.

The NASA dataset metadata explicitly provides a [public-domain label for Ceres](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/dawn-fc2-derived-ceres-mosaics-v1-0) and [government-works license for Vesta](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/dawn-fc2-derived-vesta-global-mosaics-v1-0-52040). This records the selected scientific dataset boundary; it does not imply that all DLR website images are freely licensed. Dataset references: DAWN-A-FC2-5-CERESMOSAIC-V1.0 (Roatsch et al., PDS, 2016); DAWN-A-FC2-5-MOSAIC-V1.0 (Roatsch et al., PDS, 2015).

Lanczos resizing retains the full longitude/latitude extent. Only exact-black runs connected to polar column edges are marked with alpha zero and displayed as plain grey. This conservative mask preserves interior shadows but cannot detect every internal missing patch; it is not measured coverage or area. Source resolution, gap patterns and processing hashes are retained in `artifacts/evolution/2026-10-06/round-56/source/derivatives.json`. Lighting, albedo-derived relief and scene rotation are artistic. Vesta uses an outer ellipsoid of approximate published semi-axis proportions ([NASA/JPL PIA14703](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/images/pia14703-map-of-vestas-equatorial-latitudes-and-southern-hemisphere/)); its source-derived solid is described below.

## Martian moons

`phobos.jpg` and `deimos.jpg` preserve the original 1440 × 720 grayscale [JPL Solar System Simulator maps](https://space.jpl.nasa.gov/tmaps/mars.html), assembled from Viking images processed at USGS. Credit: **NASA/JPL/USGS**, with Caltech/JPL/USGS listed as creator and owner. They are historical visualization textures, also described in NASA’s [Phobos](https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/mars-phobos/) and [Deimos](https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/mars-deimos/) texture entries, not elevation or a complete reconstructed surface. Original lighting, seams, smoothed and plain low-detail patches remain. No coverage mask is invented. The finite shared mip array may resize them, while filtering limits their effective detail to their native resolution.

The approximate ellipsoid proportions use NASA’s published diameters: [Phobos](https://science.nasa.gov/mars/moons/phobos/) 27 × 22 × 18 km; [Deimos](https://science.nasa.gov/mars/moons/deimos/) 15 × 12 × 11 km. Scene axis orientation, illumination, albedo-derived relief, enlarged sizes and orbital motion are artistic; concave crater interiors are not reconstructed. Source URLs, exact unchanged image hashes and acquisition evidence live in `artifacts/evolution/2026-10-07/round-67/source/`. NASA [media usage guidance](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/) applies, with attribution and no NASA endorsement implied.

## Small-world outlines

Phobos, Deimos and Vesta now use simplified source-derived solids, including broad concave terrain, from their attributed NASA visualization models. The former convex supports have been superseded for visible rendering and initialized CPU rays. Original map provenance, missing coverage and illustrative map orientation remain unchanged; historical maps are not registered to model UVs. Both LODs and selected/neighbour rendering share the actual solid. See [finite geometry provenance and processing](../geometry/README.md) for these and the new Eros, Bennu, Itokawa and 67P shapes, resource bounds, hashes and limitations. These assets do not create a new measured height layer.
