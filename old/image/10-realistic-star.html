Shader Study // Prototype

One star, but no toy glow.

This study uses a palette-shift surface shader, one limb shell, and restrained additive layers so the body reads like a hot, optical object instead of a cartoon circle with a bright outline.

Surface 4 Animated grayscale lookups drive the palette field and hot patches.
Outer Layers 3 Limb shell, one soft glow, one corona sprite, then bloom.

Prototype Notes

Performance stays focused on the main body.

Palette-shift surface

A grayscale solar surface texture becomes color only through ramp lookup, which keeps the hot and shadow regions coherent across profiles.

Limb glow shell

Back-side expansion around the rim replaces the old hard ring and makes the edge read more like corona leakage.

Budget discipline

Instead of stacking many full-screen effects, the template spends most of its budget on the core shader and keeps outer layers sparse.
