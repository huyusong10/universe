Deep Space Template // Observatory Study

Make the background feel like a universe, not a wallpaper.

This template is built around a layered deep-space field: sparse dark regions, a soft galactic band, brighter anchor stars, and one large knowledge sun that still owns the shot. The goal is not “more dots,” but a believable sense that the scene continues far beyond the interface.

Density Logic

Uneven on purpose

Optical Feel

Glow only where earned

Scale Cue

Foreground star, endless field

The large star suppresses nearby clutter so the far field can still read as truly distant.
